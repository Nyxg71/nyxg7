03 FAQ

Questions,
answered

Still curious? The community channels are the fastest way to reach us.

What is NYXG7?

NYXG7 is a community and project hub. It's the home base for our people and the projects we build together.

How do I get involved?

The channels listed in Channels open at launch — that's where the conversation starts. Until then, the roadmap is the schedule.

What's being built right now?

Phase 1 — Foundation: the community itself and this website. Projects and new features arrive in Phase 2.

How do the phases work?

Foundation, then Launch, then Growth. Each phase is completed in order, so the roadmap is also the release schedule.

Can I contribute?

Yes — ideas, feedback and help are all welcome. Start a conversation in the Discord server and tell us what you'd like to work on.
